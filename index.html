Content Deliverables & Arrangement

What you will receive

The following deliverables are confirmed as part of this arrangement and are not subject to change.

01
Full raw content gallery
All photos and videos captured throughout the day, sorted to remove duplicates and anything blurry, then organised by moment (getting ready · ceremony · portraits · reception), and delivered via Google Drive within 24 hours of the event.
02
1 hero highlight reel
A 60–120 second vertical edit covering the full day, music-synced and ready to post to Instagram Reels or TikTok. Delivered within 5–7 days of the event.
03
Up to 3 short-form clips
Up to 3 individual 15–30 second vertical clips, each optimised for social media. You're welcome to suggest specific moments you'd love edited - or leave it entirely to Ruby and let the content speak for itself. Delivered within 5–7 days of the event.

Reception coverage & wrap time

Late-night content quality drops significantly once the dancefloor opens and lighting dims. To protect turnaround time and content quality, we agree a clear wrap point in advance.

Content usage & permissions

The following terms are agreed upon by both parties as a condition of this arrangement. Please take a moment to read these before signing off.

Ruby's rights - Ruby may use any content captured on the day for portfolio and promotional purposes across her personal social media channels (Instagram and TikTok), website, and any future client pitches, with no time limit.

Posting timeline - Ruby will not post content to her own channels until the couple has received their delivered files. After delivery, Ruby is free to post at any time.

The couple's rights - The couple have full, unlimited rights to use, edit, re-edit, share, and post all delivered content - raw and edited - however they wish, forever, with no restrictions.

Credit & tagging - Where possible, the couple agree to tag Ruby when sharing content she has created. This is appreciated but not a hard requirement.
